### 1. What problem does this system solve? Who benefits?
- **Problem:** The homepage would otherwise be filled with trending, most-viewed videos that are not what a specific user wants to watch. Also, most of YouTube's viewership did not come from the homepage but from searches and links shared off the platform.
- **Beneficiaries:** Users and YouTube. With content tailored to each user's interests, users keep discovering new videos that match their interests, which increases usage and profit for YouTube.

### 2. Which AI stack layer is most visible to users?
- **Layer:** Application Development layer
- **Explanation:** There are options at the top of the homepage showing what kind of content to watch. The selections are curated from our viewing habits.

### 3. Quality dimensions (at least 3, with measurement method)
1. **Quality of personalisation** — Measure: does engagement with a specific video improve how well recommendations are tailored?
2. **Transparency and user control** — Measure: can users change settings such as how much data they provide, or pause, edit or delete their YouTube search and watch history?
3. **Diversity** — Measure: do recommended videos vary in creators, formats and viewpoints?

### 4. What did the system do WELL?
- YouTube creates a "Mix" playlist for the music I listen to. When I watch a music video, it automatically moves on to the next song in the mix.

### 5. What did it do POORLY?
- YouTube may recommend content that is too similar to what users have watched before. For example, after I searched for "Premier League" and watched a related video, a Champions League video appeared in my recommendations. It keeps recommending similar football content, which limits exposure to other topics.

### 6. Responsible AI risks (at least 2). Who is harmed and how?
- **Risk 1 — Filter bubble:** repeatedly recommending similar videos limits users' exposure to different topics and viewpoints.
- **Risk 2 — Privacy:** using users' viewing history may raise privacy concerns if they do not understand how their data is used.
- **Risk 3 — Harmful content:** misleading videos may be recommended and give users inaccurate information.

### 7. If you could fix ONE thing, what and why?
- Improve content diversity by recommending videos from different topics, helping users discover new interests and viewpoints.
